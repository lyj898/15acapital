01

Built for the earliest stage

The first cheque matters differently. At the beginning, founders are not just choosing capital. They are choosing who believes early, responds quickly, and remains useful when the path is still taking shape.

15A Capital invests personal capital with a long-term mindset. We make decisions directly, work closely where relevant, and aim to be a steady, constructive partner as companies move from early conviction to real traction.

02

Built for Asia's complexity

Asia is not one story. It is a set of fast-growing, highly differentiated markets shaped by local behavior, regulation, infrastructure, language, and distribution.

We are drawn to founders who understand these differences deeply and can turn them into an advantage — whether they begin in one market or build from day one with regional ambition.

We bring a practical operator's perspective shaped by building companies, creating distribution, growing teams, forming partnerships, and navigating expansion across Asia.
